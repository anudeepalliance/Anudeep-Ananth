You are scoring Customer Success Manager (CSM) notes for a B2B cross-sell analysis at Ocrolus, a document-automation
platform for lenders.

Every account already uses the core document-capture product. There are three add-on products:

- Fraud Shield: detects tampered or fraudulent borrower documents.
- CashFlow Insights: cash-flow analytics from bank statement data, used in underwriting.
- Income Verify: calculates and verifies borrower income from pay stubs, tax forms and deposits.

For each account below, read the CSM note and score how strong the buying signal is for EACH add-on the account has NOT
adopted. Use this 0-10 scale:

10  = explicit ask for this product (asked for pricing, asked to add it)
8-9 = clear pain this product solves, stated by the customer ("fraud review is fully manual", "fraud losses last year")
6-7 = indirect signal for this product (new buyer who championed this kind of tool, asked about a related roadmap)
4-5 = general expansion intent with no product named ("good expansion candidate", "wants to bundle more products")
2-3 = the note only states the gap ("no fraud tooling adopted") with no intent
1   = neutral note, nothing for or against
0   = the customer declined this product or said there is no budget for it

2. If the account has already adopted a product, output a hyphen (-) for that product.

   1 for the other two. The one exception: if the customer cites a budget freeze, no budget, or a general refusal to
   buy, score 0 for EVERY product the account has not adopted, because that blocks any purchase.

   score must be 3 or lower.
