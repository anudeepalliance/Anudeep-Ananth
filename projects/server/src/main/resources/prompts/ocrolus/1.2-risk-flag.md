You are reviewing Customer Success Manager (CSM) notes for a B2B cross-sell campaign at Ocrolus, a document-automation
platform for lenders.

Before we pitch any add-on product, we need to know which accounts should NOT be pitched right now because the
relationship itself is at risk. A cross-sell message to an unhappy or unstable account can damage the renewal.

For each account below, read the CSM note and decide whether the account is at risk.

Set flagged_as_risk = Y if the note states any of the following:

- a billing dispute or other commercial dispute
- possible or flagged non-renewal, or a renewal described as at risk
- the champion or key contact has left
- usage is declining or has dropped
- support escalations or a sharp rise in support tickets
- the account is flagged as at-risk by Customer Success

Set flagged_as_risk = N in all other cases, including:

- neutral, quiet or "unremarkable" accounts with no stated problem
- low or middling engagement with no stated problem
- a customer who declined one product or cited a budget freeze (that is a buying signal issue, not a relationship risk)
- an early-stage account whose usage is still ramping up

Rules:

1. Decide ONLY from the text of the note. Do not infer risk from the company name, vertical or any other field.
2. Do not flag an account because the note is short or lacks a positive signal. Absence of good news is not risk.
3. For every Y, quote the exact words from the note that justify it. If you cannot quote words, the answer is N.
